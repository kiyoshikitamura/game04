## Product-owner choices in dependency order

### A1 — Define the initial 20 Characters (D-02)

**Required answer**

For each initial Character, provide enough approved detail to answer all of the
following without relying on implementation assumptions:

1. Who is the Character, and what makes the Character recognizable on sight?
2. What species/ecology, everyday role, personality/action, and relationship to
   the protagonist define the Character?
3. Why can the Character participate in battle?
4. How do movement, growth, awakening, and support expression reinforce the
   Character's appeal?
5. Which facts belong in stable Character Master data, and which remain
   scenario, editorial, asset, or presentation content?
6. What is explicitly outside the first-release definition?

The answer must also approve the exact species allocation and detailed roster.
It must preserve the fixed composition and design principles unless the product
owner explicitly opens a change to the handoff.

**Why needed:** a count and design principles do not identify the subjects of
Awakening, Push/Support, content production, or the asset pipeline.

**Unlocks:** D-03 and D-04 final definition; Character contract design; initial
roster production planning. It does not unlock Gacha, growth, or production
implementation.

**Optional detail:** illustrative scenarios, reference experiences, and
anti-goals. Examples remain non-binding unless explicitly approved.

### A2 — Complete the Creative Awakening contract (D-03)

**Required answer**

Using the fixed creative-evolution direction, specify:

1. player-facing purpose and intended feeling;
2. eligibility and the player's action;
3. observable outcome at each required milestone;
4. persistence, change, or reversal behavior;
5. relationship between creative change and performance change, if any;
6. how the result expresses identity;
7. behavior that “Awakening” must not imply.

Any duplicate requirement, cost, quantity, stat effect, and exact creative asset
remains undecided until stated explicitly. The source's special-animation item
is a candidate, not an automatic requirement.

**Why needed:** the pillar has a fixed direction but its authoritative state,
trigger, exact outcomes, and delivery contract are incomplete.

**Unlocks:** a later Creative Awakening implementation contract and Character
asset delivery PoC scope. It does not approve economy, growth values, rewards,
or final art.

**Optional detail:** example player story, desired emotion, and presentation
references; they remain illustrative until approved.

### A3 — Decide Push/Support inputs and intent (D-04)

**Required answer**

Classify every source-named signal—leader setting, formation, use, growth,
awakening, home placement, and event participation—and any new proposal with:

- `COUNTS`, `DOES NOT COUNT`, or `DEFERRED`;
- the player intent it represents;
- the authoritative completion event;
- whether repetition is meaningful in principle;
- abuse or accidental-action concerns requiring later policy.

Also list actions that must never be interpreted as Support merely because they
correlate with engagement or payment.

**Why needed:** Fandom cannot be defined until intentional support is separated
from general activity. Analytics, missions, and community projections must not
infer player intent independently.

**Unlocks:** D-05 Fandom definition, later Support event contracts, and abuse
policy discovery. It does not unlock formulas, weights, thresholds, rankings,
rewards, or monetization values.

**Optional detail:** additional candidate actions may remain `DEFERRED`.
Omission never means an action counts.

### A4 — Prepare vertical-slice selection (D-19)

**Required in Batch A:** approve only the selection criteria and evidence needed
to choose 1–3 Characters. Do not select Characters before their definitions and
the dependent product rules are approved.

The criteria must explain how the selection will demonstrate:

- the Character contract from A1;
- the Creative Awakening experience from A2;
- the Push/Support entry point from A3;
- later-approved Fandom behavior;
- later-approved acquisition, duplicate, economy, and growth behavior;
- sufficient asset-pipeline variety without representing the remaining roster
  as approved.

**Why needed:** early content selection can turn examples into implicit defaults
and bias the rest of the roster.

**Unlocks now:** the selection procedure only.

**Final selection unlocks:** first playable-slice content and Character asset
delivery PoC, only after D-05 and D-12 through D-15 close.

**Optional detail:** a non-binding candidate list may be marked
`CANDIDATE — NOT APPROVED` after A1. It cannot contain inferred details.

## Partial-answer rules

- Each item may be `ANSWERED`, `PARTIALLY ANSWERED`, or `DEFERRED`.
- A partial answer unlocks only the exact sub-boundary named under `SAFE TO USE
  NOW`.
- Blank fields remain open. They never inherit an example, prior-product rule,
  industry convention, or implementation suggestion.
- Optional detail is not required to approve a complete required answer.
- New Character details or product values raised during discussion require an
  explicit decision record before implementation.
- A4 cannot be fully `APPROVED` while D-05 or any of D-12 through D-15 is open.
